ACCELERATED ARCHITECTURE ANALYSIS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Coding assistant:
The three candidates are:

1. Layered monolith: simplest deployment and transactions, but vulnerable to
   accidental coupling without stronger internal boundaries.
2. Modular monolith with hexagonal boundaries: one deployment and relational
   transaction boundary, with explicit Identity, Events, Commerce,
   Notifications, and Administration modules plus replaceable adapters.
3. Microservices/event-driven: independent scaling and deployment, but
   distributed purchase consistency, sagas, duplicate/out-of-order events,
   service security, tracing, and operational overhead.

The comparison drivers are purchase consistency, authorization, provider
replaceability, operational complexity, scaling, and future extraction.
The selected option is the modular monolith because the requirements strongly
favor reliable inventory/payment transitions and do not establish a need for
distributed deployment.
